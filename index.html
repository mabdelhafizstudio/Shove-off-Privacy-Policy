SHOVE OFF

Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Shove Off is a physics puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and how you can remove it. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Shove Off.

The short version

  • We don’t collect, store or sell any personal information. We don’t run servers that the app talks to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no accounts.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • If you play multiplayer, the name you play under and your race progress are shared with the other players, either directly over your local network or through Apple’s Game Center. They are never sent to us.

Information stored on your device

Shove Off saves a small amount of information on your device so the game remembers where you are. It is kept in the app’s own storage, is not synced to iCloud by the app, and is never sent to us.

  • Progress: the highest level you’ve cleared, how many times you’ve lost each unfinished level (used to send shield blocks after 10 losses), and your best Endless score.
  • Settings: Sound, Haptics, Gyro Levels and Board Glass.
  • Multiplayer name: the name you enter on the Multiplayer screen, up to 16 characters, if you enter one.
  • Recent taunts: the end-of-run lines you’ve seen recently, so the game doesn’t repeat them.

Motion sensor

On gyro levels, the app reads your device’s motion sensor so tilting your phone tilts the board. Readings are used only while you play, are not stored and never leave your device. You can turn this off with Settings → Gyro Levels, and the level will tilt on its own instead.

Multiplayer

You don’t need multiplayer to play. Everything else works offline and without signing in to anything.

Nearby (local network)

When you open Multiplayer, iOS asks whether Shove Off can use your local network. The app needs this to find and connect to other phones on the same Wi‑Fi. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

  • When you host a room, your device announces the room on your local network under your multiplayer name, so anyone nearby who opens Shove Off’s room list can see it.
  • When you join or play, your device connects directly to the other players’ devices. They exchange your multiplayer name, your difficulty pick, and race information such as reds knocked off, yellows left, time and result.
  • This information goes only between the devices in the room, over your local network. It is not sent to us or to any server. Because it travels within your local network, avoid using personal details in your multiplayer name.

Online (Game Center)

Online duels use Apple’s Game Center. When the app starts, it signs you in to Game Center quietly if you are already signed in on your device. Game Center is only used for online duels.

  • Quick Match or Invite a Friend asks Game Center to find you an opponent. Your opponent sees your Game Center name and your multiplayer name, and both phones exchange the same race information as in a nearby race, sent through Game Center.
  • Your Game Center player ID is used only on your device, to decide which phone runs the match.
  • Game Center is provided by Apple, and Apple handles your Game Center data under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can manage your Game Center profile, friends and sign-in in Settings → Game Center. If Screen Time restricts multiplayer games, online play is turned off.

What Shove Off doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t show ads or track you across apps or websites.
  • It doesn’t include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It doesn’t ask for or use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It doesn’t require an account and has no in-app purchases.

Crash reports from Apple

If you’ve turned on Share With App Developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us crash reports and basic usage statistics for Shove Off. Apple collects and anonymizes this information, and you can turn it off at any time in that setting. We use it only to fix bugs and make the game run better.

Children

Shove Off is suitable for players of all ages. Because the app doesn’t collect personal information, we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can use Screen Time to restrict Game Center multiplayer.

Removing your information

  • Settings → Reset Progress in Shove Off clears your level progress and best Endless score.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Shove Off has stored on your device, including your settings and multiplayer name.
  • Game Center information is managed by Apple in Settings → Game Center.

Changes to this policy

If Shove Off starts using information in a new way, we’ll update this policy and change the effective date at the top.

Contact
